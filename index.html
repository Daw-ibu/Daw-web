Why ?


Why did I create this website? Simple—because some moments are too special to be forgotten.

This website is a collection of memories, adventures, laughter, and experiences I've shared with my friends. From exploring worlds in Sky-cotl, surviving together in Wasteland, flying through Sky, or just hanging out and talking, every moment has helped create memories that I will always treasure.
